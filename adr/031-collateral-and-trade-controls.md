# ADR-031 — Collateral and trade-document controls: record, calculate, gate, never act
**Status:** Accepted for Phase 7a, **pending review by the tech lead, the credit policy owner, treasury/operations and legal**
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Context:** COL-01..04 and TRD-01..03 require that the platform never places or releases a pledge, trades, liquidates or issues an instrument, that valuations carry source and time, that the lending arithmetic follows bank-approved rules, that margin calls are tracked, that a scan never replaces an original, that discrepancies go through people, and that issuance is confirmed by the trade system. The bank has supplied no eligible-securities list, haircuts, rates, price sources, document lists or legal view.
**Decision:**
1. **Definitions are configuration approved by the credit policy owner:** `security_list` (approved valuation sources with a maximum price age, securities with eligibility, haircut, optional concentration limit), `collateral_policy` (advance rate, initial and maintenance coverage, margin-call deadline, valuation validity), `trade_checklist` (documents with required/original flags and an in-person reason, key dates with alert windows). Each names exactly one product class. Publication refuses any key that would place or release a pledge, sell, liquidate, waive a document or issue an instrument.
2. **Arithmetic** (`margin-1`) is fixed-point over recorded holdings and prices: market value, haircut, per-security concentration cap (once, without re-spreading the excess), borrowing base, coverage against requested plus existing exposure, tests against the initial and maintenance levels, shortfall to the maintenance level. A holding that should count but has no usable price (missing, older than its source allows, or from an unapproved source) makes the whole result **incomplete**. Not-listed, not-eligible and not-free-to-pledge holdings are shown and left out. Every run is stored with everything it used.
3. **Margin calls** are issued by a person on a fresh valuation that calls for one, with a note; the deadline comes from the policy; one open call per file (database). "Cured" is proven by a newer valuation meeting the maintenance level; a call may instead be closed through the bank's own process with evidence. Overdue calls are flagged once and counted.
4. **Pledge confirmations** are recorded by a role that does not value (operations), with the authorised system's reference and a note; release needs a prior confirmation. There is no pledge, release, sell, liquidate or trade route.
5. **Trade documents** are an append-only history per document; state is derived. A copy is accepted by someone other than the person who received it; an original is sighted by someone other than the person who took it in; a discrepancy needs a note and a correction before acceptance. A required original keeps the document incomplete until sighted. Key dates are flagged once inside their alert window.
6. **Gates on approval nodes** (`valuation_current_and_ok`, `no_open_margin_call`, `trade_documents_complete`) appear as checks in the decision panel and only block. Instrument issuance uses the Phase 5 hand-off: approval creates it, the customer sees "in progress" until the authorised system's reference is recorded.
**Consequences:** margin monitoring continues after approval, so the collateral actions are allowed on approved cases. The operations role that records pledges cannot open the case workspace (it holds no case-read right), so the pledge form is reachable only by a role that holds both; the demo records pledges through the API (A-053). Amendments and extensions are new cases that refer to the original by convention, not a built workflow.
**Not decided (bank):** every number and list above; legal view of electronic documents and originals; who may record pledges; price sources; margin-call procedure and wording; whether a margin call blocks other lending.
**Alternatives rejected:** letting the platform call a custody system to pledge (COL-01); a score or limit that combines securities and other products (CRD-07); treating an accepted scan as complete (TRD-01); using the last known price when a feed is down (invented value).
