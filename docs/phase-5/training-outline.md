# Staff training outline (draft, to be adapted with the bank)

Short sessions with the staff portal on synthetic data. Each ends with a practical exercise.

| Role | Topics | Exercise |
|---|---|---|
| Branch officer | Work queue and priority reasons; claiming; the case workspace; reading what the customer changed; asking the customer for more information in plain words; internal notes are never shown to customers; why you cannot approve your own work | Claim a case, ask for a missing photo, review the correction |
| Approver | Decision panel and authority checks; reading "Above your authority limit"; the decision is final and recorded with a rationale; separation from earlier stages | Approve one case, decline one, see the refusal for a case above your limit |
| Compliance officer | Clearance tasks; what happens if a clearance is missing (the workflow will not publish) | Complete a clearance |
| Operations officer | Hand-offs page; sent is not complete; recording the bank's reference and note; unclear results (never retry first); refused and stopped items; reconciliation; pilot page and baselines | Confirm a hand-off, resolve an unclear one, retry a refused one, enter a baseline |
| Team lead | Turnaround page: bank time versus customer wait, percentiles, definitions; no per-person figures | Explain a slow case from the history |
| Support staff | What customers can see; what may not be said; how to find a case by tracking code (a locator, not a password) | Answer three scripted customer questions without revealing internal information |
| Everyone | Security basics: authenticator code, no shared accounts, no real customer data in training, how to report an incident | Report a mock incident |

Materials to produce with the bank: screenshots (see `docs/evidence/phase-5/`), one-page quick guides in English and Nepali, and the runbook.
